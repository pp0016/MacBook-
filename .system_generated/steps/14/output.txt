The Apple M3 chip series is built on **TSMC’s 3nm (N3B) process node**[1][2][3]. Below are the key technical specifications for the M3 family:

### **Transistor Count**
The transistor count varies across the M3 series based on the specific chip configuration[1]:
*   **M3:** 25 billion transistors[4][5][3]
*   **M3 Pro:** 37 billion transistors[6][3]
*   **M3 Max:** 92 billion transistors[4][7][3]
*   **M3 Ultra:** 184 billion transistors[8][9]

### **Neural Engine (NPU)**
The entire M3 family features a 16-core Neural Engine[5][3]. Apple has rated the peak performance of this Neural Engine at **18 TOPS** (trillions of operations per second)[1][2][3]. 

### **Memory Bandwidth**
Memory bandwidth varies depending on the specific model within the M3 family:
*   **M3:** Up to **100 GB/s**[10][2]
*   **M3 Pro:** Up to **150 GB/s**[11]
*   **M3 Max:** Up to **300 GB/s** (base configurations may vary)[12][13][14]

***

*Note: The M3 Ultra, released in March 2025, utilizes Apple's UltraFusion architecture to combine two M3 Max dies, effectively doubling the core counts and capabilities of the M3 Max[8][9].*
