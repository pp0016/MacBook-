The **Apple M5 chip** used in the MacBook Air features a unified memory bandwidth of **153GB/s**[1][2][3]. 

Key specifications regarding the M5 memory and architecture in the MacBook Air include:

*   **Memory Architecture:** Like other Apple Silicon chips, the M5 utilizes a **unified memory** architecture where the CPU, GPU, and Neural Engine share the same memory pool[4][5].
*   **Memory Type:** It utilizes **LPDDR5X** memory[4].
*   **Memory Configurations:** The MacBook Air with M5 comes with 16GB of unified memory as the standard base configuration, with options to configure it to 24GB or 32GB[1][3][6].
*   **Performance Context:** The 153GB/s bandwidth represents a significant increase (approximately 30%) over the previous generation, designed to support improved multitasking, larger creative projects, and accelerated AI and machine learning workloads[7][8]. 

The M5 chip itself is built on TSMC’s **3nm (N3P)** process and includes a 16-core Neural Engine and dedicated neural accelerators within the GPU cores to enhance on-device AI performance[3][9][4][5].
