### **Performance Considerations**

*   **MLX Acceleration:** Ensure you are using a version of Ollama (0.19+) that supports **MLX**. This framework is specifically tuned for Apple Silicon and uses your M5’s Neural Accelerators to significantly speed up both prompt processing and token generation.
*   **The "Air" Factor:** Your MacBook Air is fanless. For short, creative bursts (writing a post, generating a quick image, summarizing a meeting), it will run at full speed. During very long, sustained tasks (like complex code refactoring or batch processing), the system may thermally throttle slightly to maintain temperature.
*   **Unified Memory:** Remember that the GPU pulls directly from your 16GB RAM. Close memory-heavy apps (like large browser sessions or video editors) if you find a specific AI model is running slowly or failing to load.
