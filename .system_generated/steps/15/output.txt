The base Apple M4 chip features the following specifications:

*   **Transistor Count:** 28 billion[1][2][3]
*   **Process Node:** TSMC second-generation 3-nanometer (N3E)[4][3][5]
*   **Cores:**
    *   **CPU:** Up to 10 cores (typically configured with 4 performance cores and 6 efficiency cores)[6][7][2]
    *   **GPU:** 8, 9, or 10 cores[1][7]
    *   **Neural Engine:** 16-core[1][6][7]
*   **Memory Bandwidth:** 120 GB/s[1][7][3]
*   **TOPS (Neural Engine):** Up to 38 trillion operations per second[1][4][2]
