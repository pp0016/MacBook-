To access your Ollama instance on a Mac mini from your MacBook Air (or any other device) from anywhere in the world, you can use **Tailscale** to create a secure, private network (tailnet) that links your devices without exposing your services to the public internet[1][2][3].

### Prerequisites
*   **Tailscale:** Installed and signed in on both your Mac mini and your MacBook Air[4][3].
*   **Ollama:** Installed on your Mac mini[1][5].

---

### Step 1: Configure Ollama for Remote Access
By default, Ollama only listens on `localhost` (127.0.0.1)[2][5]. You must configure it to listen on all interfaces so it can receive traffic from your Tailscale network[5][4].

Since Ollama on macOS runs as a background service via `launchd`, a simple environment variable in your shell profile will not work[5]. You need to set it via the system environment:

1.  Open your terminal on the Mac mini.
2.  Run the following command to set the environment variable for the launch agent:
    ```bash
    launchctl setenv OLLAMA_HOST "0.0.0.0:11434"
    ```
3.  **Restart Ollama** completely (quit the app from the menu bar and relaunch it) for the changes to take effect[5].

---

### Step 2: Connect via Tailscale
1.  **Find your Tailscale IP:** On your Mac mini, open the Tailscale menu or run `tailscale ip -4` in the terminal to find its internal Tailscale IP address (e.g., `100.x.y.z`).
2.  **Verify connection:** From your MacBook Air (while connected to Tailscale), try to reach the Ollama API by running:
    ```bash
    curl http://<MAC_MINI_TAILSCALE_IP>:11434/api/tags
    ```
    If successful, you will see a JSON response listing your downloaded models[6].

---

### Step 3: Accessing from your MacBook Air
Now that the connection is established, you have several ways to use the LLM:

*   **Direct API Usage:** Any LLM client or application on your MacBook Air can point to `http://<MAC_MINI_TAILSCALE_IP>:11434` as the base URL[4][6].
*   **Web Interface (Recommended):** For a ChatGPT-like experience, run **Open WebUI** using Docker on your Mac mini[1][7].
    *   Once Open WebUI is running (typically on port 3000), you can access it from your MacBook Air's browser at `http://<MAC_MINI_TAILSCALE_IP>:3000`[5][8].
*   **Tailscale Serve (Optional):** If you prefer not to manage ports manually, you can use `tailscale serve` to expose your local service to your tailnet with a secure URL[9][8]:
    ```bash
    tailscale serve --bg 3000
    ```
    This allows you to access your interface via a private Tailscale-provided address[9][8].

### Security Warning
**Do not forward ports (like 11434) on your router to the public internet**[10][11]. By using Tailscale, your Ollama instance remains completely private, encrypted, and accessible *only* to devices authenticated within your personal Tailscale network[2][12][3].
