For the MacBook Air with the **M5 chip**, the base model comes configured with a **10-core CPU and an 8-core GPU**[1][2][3].

*   **Chip:** Apple M5 (10-core CPU, 8-core GPU)[1][2][3]
*   **Memory:** 16GB Unified Memory (standard)[4][3]
*   **Storage:** 512GB SSD (standard)[4][3]
*   **Display:** 13.6-inch Liquid Retina display[2][3]
