The MacBook Air (M5, 2026) was released in March 2026. Below are the key specifications regarding its battery life and Neural Engine capabilities.

### **Battery Life**
The M5 MacBook Air is rated for:
*   **Up to 18 hours** of video streaming.
*   **Up to 15 hours** of wireless web browsing.
*   It utilizes a built-in 53.8-watt-hour lithium-polymer battery and supports fast charging with a 70W or higher USB-C power adapter.

### **Neural Engine & AI Performance**
The M5 chip features a significant focus on AI-driven workflows through a combination of hardware components:
*   **16-core Neural Engine:** The M5 chip retains the 16-core Neural Engine architecture[1][2], which is optimized for machine learning and on-device AI tasks.
*   **Neural Accelerators:** A notable advancement in the M5 generation is the inclusion of a **dedicated Neural Accelerator within each GPU core**[3][2]. This architecture is specifically designed to accelerate GPU-based AI workloads and Large Language Models (LLMs)[3][2].
*   **Performance Gains:** Apple claims the M5 architecture delivers up to 4x the peak GPU compute performance for AI compared to the M4 chip[2][4]. While Apple typically does not publish exact "TOPS" (Trillions of Operations Per Second) figures in their standard technical specifications, the integration of these per-core Neural Accelerators represents the primary hardware evolution for AI tasks in this generation[3][5].
