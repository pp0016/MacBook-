### **Mac mini (2026 Refresh)**
Apple refreshed the Mac mini in August 2026, skipping a standalone "M5" base model in favor of the **M6** and **M5 Pro** chips.

*   **Chip Options:**
    *   **M6 Chip:** 12-core CPU, 12-core GPU.
    *   **M5 Pro Chip:** 18-core CPU, 20-core GPU.
*   **RAM Options:**
    *   **M6 Model:** Starts at 16GB, configurable up to 32GB.
    *   **M5 Pro Model:** Starts at 24GB, configurable up to 64GB.
*   **Price in India (Starting):**
    *   **Mac mini M6:** Starts at **₹99,900** (Education price: ₹88,900).
    *   **Mac mini M5 Pro:** Starts at **₹2,09,900** (Education price: ₹1,97,900).
*   **Key Features:** Support for Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6, and Apple Intelligence (including Siri AI).

### **MacBook Air M5 (Released March 2026)**
The MacBook Air M5 continues to be the primary thin-and-light laptop offering from Apple for 2026.

*   **Chip:** Apple M5 (10-core CPU; 8-core or 10-core GPU options).
*   **RAM Options:** Starts at 16GB unified memory, configurable up to 32GB.
*   **Storage:** Starts at 512GB SSD (configurable up to 4TB).
*   **Price in India:** Prices vary by retailer and configuration, typically starting around **₹1,19,900** for the 13-inch model, with higher-end configurations (or 15-inch models) reaching **₹1,49,900** and above.
*   **Key Features:** 18-hour battery life, Liquid Retina display (13.6-inch or 15.3-inch), Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6, and support for two external displays.
