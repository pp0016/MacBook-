The Apple M5 series of chips features varying media engine capabilities and connectivity standards depending on the specific model (base, Pro, or Max).

### **AV1 and Media Engine Capabilities**
All variants of the M5 chip include a powerful Media Engine capable of hardware-accelerated **AV1 decoding**. 

Regarding **AV1 hardware encoding**, this capability is present in the **M5 Pro and M5 Max** chips, completing the full "round-trip" of hardware-accelerated support for these professional tiers.

The Media Engine specifications generally include:
*   **Decoding:** Hardware-accelerated H.264, HEVC, ProRes, ProRes RAW, and **AV1**.
*   **Encoding:** Hardware-accelerated H.264, HEVC, and ProRes.
*   **Pipeline Scaling:** The **M5 Max** doubles the media pipeline compared to the base M5 and M5 Pro, featuring two video encode engines and two ProRes encode and decode engines.

### **Thunderbolt Version**
Connectivity standards for Thunderbolt differ by chip tier:
*   **Apple M5 (Base):** Supports **Thunderbolt 4** (up to 40Gb/s).
*   **Apple M5 Pro and M5 Max:** Feature integrated **Thunderbolt 5** controllers on-chip, with each port backed by its own dedicated controller to allow for full, simultaneous bandwidth across all ports.
